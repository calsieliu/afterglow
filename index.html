AFTERGLOW is an invitation-only art fair where art is felt before it is seen. Guests move through four moments: Arrive, Discover, Gather and Linger.

Music, scent and live painting change as the evening goes on, so guests never quite know what comes next. They leave with a perfume that keeps the evening with them.
